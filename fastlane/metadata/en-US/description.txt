Texora turns your text into polished portrait slides in minutes. Paste a script, bullet list, or notes - or tap the mic and speak. Texora automatically splits your content into slide-sized chunks, then lets you style each slide with images or beautiful gradients.

Designed for social posts, story-style presentations, quotes, explainers, and quick decks, Texora keeps the workflow simple: write -> split -> style -> export.

What you can do:
- Convert long text into multiple slides with smart splitting and slide count estimates
- Add a background to every slide from your photo library or choose built-in gradients
- Edit slide text before you design, and keep everything aligned to your message
- Fine-tune typography: fonts, colors, background opacity, and size
- Apply eye-catching text effects like glow, shadows, bloom, and 3D styles
- Drag to position text and pinch to resize for fast visual control
- Use undo/redo and auto-save so you never lose work
- Preview the full slide sequence before exporting
- Export high-resolution PNG slides (up to 1080px) directly to Photos

Texora also supports 30+ interface languages and multiple themes, so it feels native wherever you work.

Whether you're creating story slides, carousel-style posts, or lightweight presentations, Texora helps you go from idea to shareable visuals - fast.
